For Program Graduates

Dedicated Dads 2.0

Ongoing mentoring and practical help for dads who have turned a corner and are building a constructive life.

Even after the men have turned a corner, major hurdles can remain: transportation to work, safe housing where children can stay overnight, and funds for legal help to gain rights with their children.

Dedicated Dads 2.0 directly helps graduates overcome those obstacles after classroom graduation and acceptance of ongoing mentoring.
