We Help Dads

Who We Are

We prepare troubled dads to better understand their fatherhood role and help them through the life changes needed to become constructive and fully involved dads.

Our Work

Connection, preparation, and practical help.

01

Influence

We provide guidance, information, and testimonials to influence dads to make the life changes needed to be present for their children.

02

Prepare

We give men fatherhood instruction and a plan for becoming the dads their children need.

03

Help

We connect committed dads with mentoring, legal assistance, jobs, and social-service support.
